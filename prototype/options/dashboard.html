  • Kept together means an image, a form object (a drawing that the PDF stores as one unit), or the shapes of one tagged figure. Without tags, images are still compared, and the SVG that Typst draws inline falls apart into loose shapes.
  • Loose shapes are everything else that is drawn. Typst marks a diagram's shapes exactly as it marks table rules and underlines, and nothing says which shapes belong together. The prototype groups shapes that touch and drops groups made only of rules.
  • That grouping has two faults that show above: a highlighter's rectangle counts as a drawing, and one diagram can come apart into several.
  • Only Typst output was tried. I have not run a PDF from LaTeX or Word through either option, so form objects are untested.
